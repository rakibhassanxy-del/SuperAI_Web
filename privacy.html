Privacy Policy for Super AI Typing

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy. Super AI Typing collects minimal personal information necessary to provide our services. This includes:

  • Phone Number: Used solely as a unique identifier for account authentication and subscription management.
  • Transaction ID (TrxID): Collected exclusively for verifying subscription payments.

2. Microphone and Voice Data

Our application requires microphone access to provide core Voice-to-Text and Translation functionalities. Your voice data is processed directly via secure APIs in real-time and is NEVER recorded, stored, or saved on our servers.

3. Data Security and Usage

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Authentication and subscription data are securely stored using Google Firebase with strict database rules.

4. Microsoft Store Compliance

This application complies strictly with Microsoft Store policies. We ensure a secure, sandboxed environment for your productivity without compromising your local system's security.
